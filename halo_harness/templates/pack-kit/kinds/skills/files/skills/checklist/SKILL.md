description: The items every __NAME__ draft is checked against, each answered ok, missing or wrong.
---
Check the draft against each item and answer it `ok`, `missing` or `wrong`, with a one-line note:

- title: one line, says what the draft is
- plan: three steps at most
- request: the draft does what the request asked, and nothing else
- questions: open questions are listed, or "none"
