# Changelog

## 1.0.1 (Halo 3.5.0.0)

Fleet left Halo: the coach no longer reads a fleet mission (`halo fleet mission coach`, `halo coach mission`,
the mission's coach route) and the library no longer fetches another machine's entry (`halo coach library fetch`).

## 1.0.0 (Halo 3.4.0.0)

The coach leaves Halo's core for this pack: `halo coach`, `halo improve`, `/coach` and `/improve`, the coach probes and
the improvement library, the Coach panel in Studio's Agents section (where the run report's Coach tab and the
mission's Coach face went), its Settings rows under "From packs" and its doctor row.
