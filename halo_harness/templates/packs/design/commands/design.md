---
description: Open Studio's Design section (the design pack); /design url prints its address
argument-hint: "[url]"
studio: design
---
`/design` opens Halo Studio on the design pack's Design section: projects, the thread of design turns, artboards with
their versions, comments and knobs, the design system, exports and the handoff. `/design url` prints the address
instead of opening a browser (over SSH: forward the port and open the same URL). The same page is
`halo studio --mode design--design`, or the Design row of Studio's rail.
